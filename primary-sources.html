Primary Sources — Canadian Federal Accountability Records

Primary Sources — Canadian Federal Accountability Records. Public-record file on TENET5 — sources cited on the page.

Editorial standard. This page indexes primary sources only — official government databases, statutory oversight bodies, and publicly tabled records. It does not make allegations about any individual. Citations should be drawn directly from these databases, with file numbers, dates, and quoted text preserved.

1 Parliament & Legislative Record

Hansard — House of Commons Debates

Verbatim transcript of every sitting of the House since 1867. Searchable by member, date, bill, or phrase.

ourcommons.ca — Hansard →

Status, text, and legislative summaries for every bill before Parliament, plus sponsor and committee history.

parl.ca — LEGISinfo →

Committee Reports & Evidence

Verbatim transcripts (Evidence) and tabled reports from every standing, special, and joint committee.

ourcommons.ca — Committees →

Senate Hansard, committee evidence, and tabled reports.

sencanada.ca →

ourcommons.ca — Members → · openparliament.ca — Votes →

ourcommons.ca — Order Paper →

Citation Discipline

  • Always cite by primary record (Hansard date + speaker, OAG report §, OIC number, court citation, lobbying registration ID, ATIP file number).
  • Quote verbatim; bracket any insertions; preserve redactions as published.
  • Distinguish findings (made by an oversight body) from allegations (made by a party). Do not collapse the two.
  • Where a record is contested or under appeal, say so on the line.
