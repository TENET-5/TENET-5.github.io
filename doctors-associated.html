🔍 INVESTIGATION — MEDICAL PROFESSIONALS

Medical Professionals Named in the s.504 Information

This page documents the medical professionals named in Daniel Perry's Form 2 Information under s.504 of the Criminal Code of Canada. Every allegation below is pleaded, not yet adjudicated. Primary source: the s.504 Information itself, on record via s504-tracker.html.

Read this before continuing

What this page is. A publicly searchable index of the medical professionals named as subjects of Daniel Perry's Form 2 Information under s.504 of the Criminal Code of Canada. The Information lays 29 counts against Capt. Rebecca Covey (CFNIS) and others; the "others" include named medical professionals alleged to have participated in the pleaded misconduct.

What this page is NOT. A conviction record. A finding of fact. A substitute for court proceedings or regulatory investigation. None of the persons named below have been found guilty of any criminal offence. None of the persons named have been subject to a regulatory finding of professional misconduct on the specific allegations pleaded. The pleaded allegations remain untested.

Why this page exists anyway. The s.504 Information is filed. It is a public document. The allegations in a filed Information are, by the operation of s.504 itself, part of the public record. TENET5 is a journalism-in-public investigation; the function of this page is to index the names and the specific counts alleged so that the Information is searchable by anyone reviewing it — including the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario, MPCC, CFNIS, and any future court.

Correction policy. Any named person who believes a statement here is inaccurate or unfairly characterised beyond what the s.504 Information itself alleges may request a correction. TENET5 will update the page within 48 hours of verified notice from the named person's counsel. Contact via the About page.

Per the Information — named medical professionals

The named individuals below are the medical professionals Perry's s.504 Information specifically identifies as having participated in the pleaded misconduct. Each card shows: name and role, the specific conduct alleged (per the Information), and which of the 29 counts the allegation anchors to. The 29 counts themselves are tracked at s504-tracker.html and charges-sheet.html.

PLEADED s.504 FILED RESPONSE PENDING

Dr. Zoe Selhi

Psychiatrist — treating physician per the Information
Alleged conduct (per the s.504 Information): Medical record falsification; administration of forced psychiatric confinement and medication in connection with the broader CFNIS retaliation campaign alleged against Perry after he reported suspected foreign interference within the Canadian Armed Forces.
Counts anchored: Count 21 — medical record falsification Count 22 — forced psychiatric confinement & medication CC s.137 — fabricating evidence CC s.269.1 — torture (forced medication framing)
Status: The Information is filed. No response received from Dr. Selhi or her counsel at the time of this writing. No regulatory finding from the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario (CPSO) on the s.504 allegations has been issued.
Regulator of record: College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario (cpso.on.ca) — any regulatory complaint on the pleaded conduct would be filed here.

Note. The Crown Prosecutor named in Perry's s.504 Information (Vicky Jahye Bae) and the CFNIS lead (Capt. Rebecca Covey) are not medical professionals and are documented on a separate dossier: s504-covey-bae.html. This page indexes only medical practitioners named in the Information.

NDA SECTIONS APPLY s.504 FILED RESPONSE PENDING

CFNIS clinical liaison chain (unnamed individual officers)

Canadian Forces National Investigation Service — clinical interface
Context: Perry's s.504 Information names Capt. Rebecca Covey as the lead target and alleges that clinical processes (the psychiatric confinement order, the medication regime) were coordinated through CFNIS channels. The clinical liaison officers between CFNIS and civilian medical staff are implicated by the NDA-framing of the Information even where not individually named in the available copy.
Relevant NDA sections (duties CFNIS officers owe): NDA s.83 — duty to investigate NDA s.92 — ill-treatment of subordinate NDA s.95 — cruelty / disgraceful conduct NDA s.119 — scandalous conduct NDA s.124 — negligent performance of duty
Regulator of record: Military Police Complaints Commission (MPCC) for the CFNIS-side actors; the Provost Marshal's office for internal military-police discipline. TENET5 will track any response.

How a reader should use this page

The correct use of this page is as a starting index, not as an evidentiary record. If you are:

  • A journalist: request the s.504 Information via the Ontario court registry that received it, confirm each count directly, reach each named person for comment before publishing anything new.
  • A regulator (CPSO, Ontario Nursing, etc.): file a complaint request for each named professional and initiate your own fact-finding. The s.504 Information itself is a credible triggering document for a regulatory inquiry.
  • A lawyer advising a named person: this page does not claim what is true; it indexes what is pleaded. The correction policy in the banner above is real. Contact via the About page and a correction will be applied within 48 hours of verified notice.
  • A member of the public: understand that a filed s.504 Information is one step in a long legal process. What is pleaded today may be amended, withdrawn, upheld, or dismissed. Follow the case via s504-tracker.html.
