How to read these together

Each series alone is a public-health or fiscal story. Together they describe a state that expands death pathways and drug losses while accelerating intake faster than housing, healthcare, and institutional capacity can absorb.

  • MAID — fiscal and clinical framing of expanded eligibility; see Health Canada annual reports on the MAID file.
  • Opioids — death registration and public-health surveillance; the curve is the failure.
  • Intake — IRCC levels plans and non-permanent resident stocks vs. housing starts and backlog data.
  • Continuity — fertility, death, and migration netted into generational pressure; open fertility and veterans files for the components.
Interpretation · labeled

What the board shows. On present public telemetry — MAID expansion, unmitigated toxicity deaths, veteran neglect in departmental data, fertility collapse, and intake scaled past infrastructure capacity — the joint trajectory is a sustained drain on generational continuity paired with rapid demographic reconstitution.

What that is not. A chart is not a court finding. TENET5 maps the public record: parliamentary votes, Health Canada reports, death registration series, IRCC publications. Claims that go beyond those sources stay on the analysis side of the line and must be checked against the linked files.
