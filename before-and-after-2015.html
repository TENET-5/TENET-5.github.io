A Decade of Documented Decline

2015 vs. 2025. Twelve metrics. Every number from official data. The question is not whether Canada declined — but whether it was by design.

11 of 12 Metrics Worsened

MAID: 0 → 76K. Debt: doubled. Housing: unaffordable. Military: collapsed. Ethics: zero consequences. GDP per capita: declining.

The one improvement (boil water advisories) cost $8.2B and still has 28 remaining. If every metric worsens over a decade, is it accidental — or architectural?
