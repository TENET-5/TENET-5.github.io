Method in one breath

Items come from the multi-source RSS cycle (news_feed + home wire + headline cache). Stories are clustered by shared title tokens across outlets. Frames are descriptive tags from headline language (process, horse race, accountability, security, economy, health, foreign, human). Omissions are relative: desk topics covered by some outlets but not others, or missing from the external wire while TENET5 holds a file. No invented facts. Powered by LIRIL AI. You verify.
