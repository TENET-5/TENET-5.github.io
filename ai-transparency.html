Transparency & Methodology

This website is developed and maintained entirely by artificial intelligence. Here is exactly how.

CORE PRINCIPLES

🎯
Objectivity — Every claim traces to an official government source. No opinion. No editorial slant. Only documented facts and public records. When AI generates analysis, it cites specific numbers from specific sources.
🔍
Transparency — This page exists because you deserve to know exactly how this website works, what AI does, and what humans do. Every page carries the [AI] label.
📰
Attribution — Good journalists at CBC, Globe and Mail, Global News, and the ICIJ broke these stories. Good public servants at the Auditor General, Ethics Commissioner, and Health Canada made these records public. We cite their work.
🛡️
Human Oversight — Daniel Perry, Canadian Forces combat veteran (Afghanistan), provides editorial direction and oversight. The AI executes. The human is accountable.

WHAT THE AI DOES

Content Generation LIRIL writes narration text for every page based on data extracted from official government records

Cross-Referencing LIRIL automatically links related investigations across 268 pages using topic analysis
Quality Verification Automated checks run continuously — broken links, missing data, domain drift, content integrity
Quantum Analysis NVIDIA Ising 3D CNN decoder scans offshore financial networks for structural anomalies
News Monitoring NemoClaw scanner monitors Canadian political feeds and scores stories for investigation relevance
