Contact

TENET5 is a citizen-built dossier with no staff, no email list, no contact form, and no analytics. The site does not collect information from visitors. The channels below are the only correct routes for the most common reasons people want to get in touch.

The point of the dossier is to be checkable, not to be reachable. If a claim on this site is wrong, the right response is a public correction with a primary source — not a private message.

Channels

Channel 1

Corrections + source additions

Open an issue on the public GitHub repository. The repo is at github.com/tenet-5/tenet-5.github.io/issues. Include the URL of the page, the specific claim or sentence that is wrong or out of date, and a link to the primary source that supports the correction. The site is built from the same repository — fixing it in the open is the intentional design.

Channel 2

Citizens with prosecution-relevant information

If you have evidence that should be in front of a court rather than in a journalist's inbox, the file s.504 page walks through the Criminal Code section 504 private-prosecution pathway. The 504 database is the working record of named individuals against whom a citizen-laid information could be brought.

Channel 3

Journalists

The press kit page contains the dossier summary, key statistics with primary-source citations, the legal frame (UN Convention Article II + Rome Statute Articles 6/7/25), and contact for the citizen who built the dossier. The press kit is intended to be quoted and republished — every figure on it is independently verifiable.

Channel 4

Political staff / MPs / parliamentary committees

The MP accountability grid is the working record of votes, committee appearances, and disclosure history per Member. The one-pager is the printable version designed to be handed across a constituency-office desk in person.

Channel 5

Researchers / academics / lawyers

The methodology page documents how every figure on this site is derived; the genocide-evidence page is the legal-analysis spine; the dolus specialis page covers the specific-intent inference framework. Every cited tribunal judgment is independently retrievable from the IRMCT (ICTR/ICTY) and ICJ public archives at no charge.

Channel 6

Whistleblowers and protected disclosures

This site is not a secure intake channel. Do not email documents to anyone associated with the dossier. The right channel for protected disclosures in Canada is the Office of the Public Sector Integrity Commissioner; for journalist contact, go through the press kit and use whatever Signal / SecureDrop channel that publication operates. Material the citizen- built site cannot legally hold should not be sent to the citizen-built site.

What this site is not

Not social media. Not a comments platform. Not a tip line. Not a fundraising page. Not a newsletter. Not anonymous. The operator's identity is on the about page in the open because that is what citizen-built investigative work requires under Criminal Code section 504: the laying of an information by a named individual.
