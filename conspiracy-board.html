🔍 INVESTIGATION METHODOLOGY — How This Board Was Built

📊 Data Sources

Every node on this board traces to public government records: Hansard transcripts, Lobbying Commissioner Registry, Elections Canada filings, RCMP public reports, court decisions, Access to Information releases, and Auditor General findings. Zero speculation — all sourced.

🔍 Algorithmic Organization

All entities are clustered and grouped based on frequency of interaction. Connections are weighted by co-occurrence in official records — financial ties, lobbying contacts, vote correlations.

🗂 Entity Categories

Persons: MPs, Senators, lobbyists, executives.
Organizations: CIJA, CCC, CCP-linked entities, PACs.
Events: Votes, meetings, financial transfers.
Evidence: Documents, filings, testimony.
