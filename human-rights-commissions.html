Notes

  • Coverage scope. This page lists statutory human rights commissions and tribunals only. Federal sectoral bodies that adjudicate discrimination claims under their own statutes (e.g. Canadian Industrial Relations Board for federally-regulated workplaces, Federal Public Sector Labour Relations and Employment Board) are out of scope here.
  • Acronyms. Bilingual federal acronyms are shown with the English form first followed by the French form (e.g. CHRC · CCDP).
  • Statute citations. Citations refer to the consolidated statute in force at publication. Where a body's enabling Act has been replaced (e.g. Saskatchewan's 2018 recodification), the current statute is cited.
  • Editorial silence. Per M19 this page does not flag bodies as captured, conflicted, or otherwise compromised. Cross-references to oversight findings and complainant-experience data live on dedicated deep-dive pages and are deliberately omitted here.
