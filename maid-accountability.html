Lobby polarity · registry + Senate

Who pushed expansion — and who sat at the design table.

Two different tracks on the public record. Dying With Dignity Canada lobbied for broader access — densest Senate contacts in our registry scan: Sen. Pamela Wallin (advance-request bills S-248 / S-231) and Sen. Stan Kutcher (mental-illness eligibility). Separately, CIJA testified on Bill C-14 (May 2016) for conscience limits and a narrow adult end-of-life frame — not a MAID subject line in 2024 detailed filings. Foreign-jurisdiction experts (Oregon, Netherlands context, US psychiatry) also shaped the C-14 hearing record. Overlap is influence and capture — not a single foreign-state authorship claim.

  • DWDC — 136 communications in the federal Lobbying Registry export; Justice Minister’s Office among top institutions. Subject codes dominated by Justice (SMT-26) and Health (SMT-18).
  • Wallin — sponsor of Senate MAID advance-request bills S-248 / S-231; top DWDC DPOH hit (10 contacts).
  • Kutcher — public record of pushing MAID for sole mental illness; cites Carter as not excluding mental illness (5 DWDC contacts).
  • CIJA / Marceau — C-14 Senate panel: conscience + vulnerable + palliative; Jewish Federations advocacy arm — design-table polarity, not DWDC expansion.

Decision chain · Canadian actors

Who opened the gate.

The statute path is Canadian: court → Parliament → cabinet non-appeal → expansion bill → provincial delivery. Foreign experts and a foreign-policy-adjacent lobby sat at the C-14 design table. That is influence on the record — not proof that a foreign state wrote the Criminal Code.

  1. Carter (SCC, 6 Feb 2015) — struck the absolute ban; ordered Parliament to legislate. 2015 SCC 5.
  2. C-14 (Jun 2016) — federal MAID frame; Senate LCJC pre-study heard foreign-jurisdiction medicine + CIJA.
  3. Truchon (QCCS, 11 Sep 2019) — struck “reasonably foreseeable natural death.” 2019 QCCS 3792.
  4. Non-appeal (2019–20) — federal Justice/PMO chose legislation over defending RFND at the SCC. Canadian hinge.
  5. C-7 (Mar 2021) — removed RFND; created Track 2. Disability orgs and UN disability rapporteur opposed.
  6. Scale (2016–24) — 76,475 cumulative · 16,499 in 2024 · 5.1% of deaths (Health Canada Sixth Annual).

Portfolio eras on the architecture: Justice Wilson-Raybould / Lametti · Health reporting chain through Philpott → Hajdu → Duclos → Holland → Michel · Senate Government Rep Gold on C-7 floor.

  • Dr. Linda Ganzini — Oregon Health & Science University (US Death With Dignity research into Canadian design talk).
  • Dr. Derryck Smith — testimony context from Amsterdam / Netherlands MAID jurisdiction.
  • Dr. Scott Kim — University of Michigan / US psychiatry capacity expertise.
  • Dr. K. Sonu Gaind — CPA president; caution on mental-illness-only MAID (Hong Kong dial-in = travel location, not PRC command).
  • Richard Marceau / CIJA — conscience + narrow adult end-of-life balance (Israel-policy-adjacent Canadian lobby — not Knesset authorship).
