Griffon Limited Life Extension — $800M pause.

In late June 2026, Ottawa paused the RCAF CH-146 Griffon Limited Life Extension (GLLE) implementation — a program budgeted at up to $800 million after a definition phase of up to $90 million. DND says the pause allows reassessment for cost, schedule, complexity, and planning underway for a replacement fleet. This file freezes the public record and places the pause in the four-point defence-procurement velocity cluster.

Analysis · labeled inference

Why it matters for the record.

GLLE is not an isolated delay story. Prior TENET5 briefs already tracked sole-source and accelerated defence buys (Arctic OTHR framing, submarine competition timeline, L3Harris/Saab GlobalEye). A life-extension pause that explicitly cites replacement fleet planning adds a pause/pivot mode: money and schedule pressure can convert a capped $800M sustainment path into a multibillion-dollar replacement path without a clear public options analysis. That is the investigative core — not whether helicopters need upgrades.
