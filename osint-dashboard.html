03The Paradox Pattern

The OSINT data reveals a repeating mathematical pattern across multiple MPs:

  1. Explicitly advocate for the interests of a foreign state (Israel or China) inside the Canadian Parliament
  2. Simultaneously demand investigations into "foreign interference" — always targeting a different foreign state
  3. Deploy identity politics to shield their advocacy from scrutiny
  4. Attack whistleblowers who identify the pattern — calling them "domestic terrorists" or "conspiracy theorists"
