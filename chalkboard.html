Public Chalkboard

Canadian visitors can sign in with Google, write or draw directly on the board, and use the wipe brush to clear any mark. Every stroke is tied to the signed-in account and automatically expires after one hour.

Designed for touch and mouse input on mobile and desktop.

Google sign-in required

To write on the board, users must sign in with Google and appear to be using a Canadian account context or Canadian device locale.

Board is view-only until sign-in is complete.

Board rules

  • Marks last for one hour.
  • Only signed-in Canadian Google users can draw.
  • The wipe brush can clear anyone’s marks.
  • Typed chalk notes can be placed directly on the board.
  • On local preview, a demo mode unlocks so the chalk texture can be tested before live auth is configured.
