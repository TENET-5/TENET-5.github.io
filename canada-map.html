🕸️ Corruption Networks — Cross-Municipal Patterns

These patterns span multiple municipalities and reveal systemic, coordinated failures in Canadian municipal governance.

SNC-Lavalin / Federal Procurement Network
Montréal Ottawa LRT Hamilton LRT Calgary Federal Crown Corps
SNC-Lavalin (now AtkinsRéalis) appears as vendor or sub-contractor across federal and municipal LRT projects. Corporate deferred prosecution agreement (2019) covered $127.7M in fraud on Libyan contracts. Federal political interference in SNC prosecution documented by Wilson-Raybould.
Developer Donation Web
Vancouver ABC $1.3M Brampton developers Mississauga McCallion era Calgary East Village TIF
Development industry systematically funds municipal election campaigns in exchange for favourable zoning, upzoning, and TIF financing. Vancouver's $1.3M ABC donations came despite 2018 provincial donation reform. Pattern repeats from Halifax to Victoria.
LRT Cost Overrun Pattern — Systemic
Ottawa $2.1B Toronto Crosstown $12.5B Hamilton $130M cancelled Edmonton $3.1B Calgary Green Line
Every major Canadian LRT project has gone dramatically over budget and schedule. Common contractors, shared consultants (Stantec, AECOM, WSP), and federal cost-sharing agreements create perverse incentives to lowball initial estimates. Ottawa inquiry found systemic management failures; Toronto Crosstown contractor placed in default 2023.
Coordinated Police Budget Expansion
Vancouver +19% Ottawa +9.2% Toronto +7% Hamilton 34% of tax levy Thunder Bay OHRT finding
Police budgets have expanded faster than any other municipal service category while civilian oversight mechanisms are simultaneously defunded or weakened. Thunder Bay's 2018 OHRT systemic racism finding shows accountability gap. Coordinated growth suggests lobbying by police associations.
Charbonneau Commission Spillover — QC
Montréal (Vaillancourt) Laval ($8M bribes) Longueuil Mascouche Rizzuto network
The 2015 Charbonneau Commission documented a province-wide Mafia-construction-political nexus. Laval Mayor Vaillancourt ran a 2.5% kickback scheme for 20+ years. Despite the commission, oversight mechanisms were subsequently weakened and new procurement irregularities have emerged in REM transit contracts.
Executive Pay / Sunshine List Inflation
Toronto 4,500 staff $100K+ Calgary CAO $454K Ottawa + Winnipeg
Executive compensation at Canadian cities has grown 2-3x faster than inflation over 10 years. The Ontario Sunshine List (salaries >$100K) has become a baseline rather than an exception in large cities. CUPE and management salary benchmarking creates self-reinforcing pay escalation.
