Section 07 · Outcome model

What the numbers support

  1. J1 — Critical/High is not rare. 16/30 (53%) of the sample is Critical or High. Under a null of “isolated bad apples,” this concentration is hard to reconcile without shared institutions (procurement law, transit funding, campaign finance).
  2. J2 — Category mass is procurement and transparency. Procurement / sole-source tags 21 cities; Transparency gaps tags 17. Transit (13) and integrity (14) form the next tier.
  3. J3 — Size correlates but does not monopolize risk. Score×population r=+0.69. Mid-size Ontario and northern High cases (Belleville, Iqaluit, Thunder Bay) show risk outside megacity fiscal systems.
  4. J4 — Missing findings are data gaps. 4 cities have zero discrete findings but still carry composite scores and category tags. Treat them as censored observations, not controls.
  5. J5 — Québec Critical pole is high-leverage. Two Critical observations (Montréal 91, Laval 83) dominate the provincial mean — classic leverage points. National inference should not collapse to those two rows alone.

Section 10 · Method

Data model, scoring, limits

Unit. Municipality (n=30). Fields: score, badge, province, population, budget_b, categories[], findings[] (title, detail, source, severity), shared_vendors[].

Score. Composite 0–100 from documented severity and finding load (procurement, transit, integrity, transparency, police, developer influence). Badges: Critical ≥80, High 60–79, Moderate ~45–59, Monitored below. Scores are not survey opinion and not criminal guilt.

Stats in this page. Mean, median, population std. dev., Pearson r on extract pairs, group means by province and population tier, category prevalence rates, multi-city vendor counts. No machine-learning classifier; no out-of-sample prediction.

Selection bias. Curated national sample biased toward known risk and available audits. Not a census of all Canadian municipalities. Zero-finding rows may reflect thin public extracts.

Sources. City/provincial AGs, commissions (Charbonneau, Hourigan), OHRT/OIPRD, Elections BC/Ontario, sunshine lists, council books, named reporting that cites primary documents. Each finding line carries a source.

Corrections: community desk · whistleblower guide · methodology · Related: network analysis, follow the money, primary sources, provincial analysis.
