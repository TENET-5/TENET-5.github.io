Structural finding · concentrated decision days

Canadian cabinet shuffles intentionally concentrate multiple portfolio changes on a single date — for political reasons, optics, and press-cycle management. The accountability consequence is that each change is individually reviewable by the relevant oversight body, but the aggregate cross-portfolio pattern of the shuffle itself is not anyone's jurisdiction.

On 2023-07-26, 9 axes had portfolio changes. No body reviewed them as a cluster. On 2021-10-26, 6 axes had changes. Same gap. The cross-axis finding plus the temporal- concentration finding together name this gap as structural and repeating, not accidental.
