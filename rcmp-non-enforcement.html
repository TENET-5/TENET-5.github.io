The Pattern Is Structural

Eight documented instances. Eight non-prosecutions. The RCMP consistently receives evidence of criminal activity involving political actors and declines to lay charges. This is not a resource issue — the RCMP has a $5.7B annual budget and 30,000+ employees. It is a structural issue: the national police force reports to the government it would need to investigate. When the subject of the investigation is also the employer, prosecution becomes structurally impossible.

This is why the s.504 private prosecution framework exists — it bypasses the RCMP entirely and places the Information directly before a Justice of the Peace. And when the Crown stays the private prosecution (as it inevitably will), that stay itself becomes the evidence for the ICC complementarity argument.
