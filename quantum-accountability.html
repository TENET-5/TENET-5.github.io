          06 Methodology & Limitations

          The TENET5 quantum bridge uses numpy statevector simulation on CPU. It is not a physical quantum computer. On a 4-qubit or 11-qubit register, classical brute force is trivial — Grover's quadratic speedup is not materially faster on this scale. The value of running real quantum algorithms on real data is:

          • Portability to hardware. The same circ_grover_search function runs unchanged on GPU-accelerated simulators and on real NISQ hardware (IBM Quantum, IonQ, Rigetti) given a backend adapter. Architectural readiness matters more than present-day speed.
