Malice Doctrine — Act II of V

Killing Members of the Group

Rome Statute, Article 6(a). Ontario MAiD Death Review Committee. Track 2 case files.

Article 6(a): Killing members of an identifiable group with intent to destroy that group in whole or in part. Track 2 — created by Bill C-7 in 2021 — is being applied to disabled, chronically ill, and economically vulnerable Canadians. Ontario's Chief Coroner has reviewed the cases.
