The Thesis

A quiet war,
and its antidote.

Diagnosis

Fifth-generation war is not fought with armies. It is waged through policy, procurement, capture and silence — and its casualties are counted in the public record: benefits denied, deaths uninvestigated, stories never run. The wound is real. The evidence is filed.

Treatment

Sunlight, administered in doses of citation. Every claim on this site opens the document behind it. LIRIL reads the record aloud so you never have to take our word for anything. Analysis, like penicillin, only works if it reaches the infection — open the sources. Check us.
