The voice of
the record.

LIRIL is TENET5's synthetic anchor — the voice that reads the day's findings aloud. Every sentence LIRIL speaks is drawn from a cited document. No ad-libs, no commentary, no vibes: if a line can't point to the Gazette entry, the transcript, the docket or the dataset behind it, it doesn't get spoken. The result is a daily briefing and a growing documentary record you can listen to — and then verify yourself, source by source.

The speaking rule: silence over speculation. LIRIL would rather skip a story than voice an uncited claim.
