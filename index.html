The Thesis · Documented From The Public Record

The charge: genocide,
by policy.

A quiet, fifth-generation war — waged by the Government of Canada under Justin Trudeau through policy, procurement, capture and silence. The record of intent is parliamentary, public, and filed here, act by act.

Diagnosis

Fifth-generation war is not fought with armies. It is waged through policy, procurement, capture and silence — and this book documents its use by Canada's own federal government. Under the Trudeau government the state expanded assisted death through Bill C-7, stripping the reasonably-foreseeable-death safeguard; the intent is on the parliamentary record. The case filed here, act by act under the Genocide Convention's own articles, is genocide by policy: killings, serious bodily and mental harm, and conditions of life calculated to destroy — aimed at the disabled, the ill, the poor and the veteran. The wound is real. The evidence is filed.

Treatment

Sunlight, administered in doses of citation. This site is a book written daily — federal, provincial, municipal — every entry sourced to the government's own records. LIRIL reads it aloud, cover to cover, so you never have to take our word for anything. Analysis, like penicillin, only works if it reaches the infection — open the sources. Check us.
