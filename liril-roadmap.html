How this page works

Any LIRIL advice that is substantive (not just classifier metadata) gets logged here with three possible statuses:

  • SHIPPED — a deliverable has been produced and is live on the site
  • BACKLOG — the recommendation is noted but not yet built; any investigator can pick it up
  • OPERATIONAL — infrastructure/ops advice, not content work

Each row carries the original request channel, elapsed milliseconds, and the exact reply text — so the chain between LIRIL's output and the site's content is change-detectable. The full consultation archives are at data/liril_consultation.json and data/liril_consultation_r2.json with self_sha256.

This is an attempt at transparent AI-assisted investigation: the AI doesn't author — it advises. The human investigator decides what to ship. The roadmap makes that decision trail public.
