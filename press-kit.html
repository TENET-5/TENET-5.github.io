Methodology

How TENET5 Sources Its Claims

Every claim on TENET5 is sourced from official government records: Health Canada reports, Statistics Canada databases, Auditor General audits, court decisions, parliamentary Hansard, Ethics Commissioner findings, corporate SEC filings, OECD comparative data, and peer-reviewed academic publications. We do not use anonymous sources. We do not publish unverified claims. Every page includes a source note listing specific documents. We encourage independent verification of every statistic.

What TENET5 Is and Is Not

TENET5 IS: An investigative journalism project that compiles publicly available government data into a navigable evidence chain documenting institutional capture and accountability failure. Powered by LIRIL AI for voice narration and OSINT data aggregation.

TENET5 IS NOT: A political party. A lobbying organization. An opinion site. We do not endorse candidates, parties, or policies. We document institutional performance from official data and leave conclusions to the reader.

Corrections log · transparent by design

Two hallucinations caught and corrected (2026-04-18)

TENET5 uses LIRIL to draft some campaign letter bodies. Two early- round LIRIL-drafted claims were factually incorrect and have been corrected:

  • CSC letter: "exceeding CAD 60 billion" (outdated) → "exceeding CAD 77 billion (2021 PBO headline)". Commit c67caa83.
  • Climate ECCC letter: "Federal Carbon Pricing Progress Report - 2021" (fabricated document name) → "Annual Report under the Greenhouse Gas Pollution Pricing Act". Commit 9798d8e9.

Each fixed campaign carries a hallucination_correction metadata field in data/grover_target_campaigns.json with original error, corrected value, detection method, and timestamp. An automated pre-commit gate now enforces ≥3 fact probes at 100% pass rate on any future LIRIL-drafted content. Full case log: LIRIL roadmap → · Limitations & Critiques →
