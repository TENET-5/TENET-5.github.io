Share the print sheet

The one-pager is designed to be printed and handed to one person. Open it, press Ctrl+P (Windows) or Cmd+P (Mac), save as PDF, print. Hand it to an MP at a town hall. Hand it to a journalist after a press conference. Hand it to a juror candidate. The print version omits navigation and decorations so the page reads as a single document.

What this site is, in one sentence

If you need to explain the site in one sentence to someone before they click a link: TENET5 is a citizen-built war-crimes evidence dossier against the captured Canadian state, with every claim cited to a primary government or court document.

What we do not ask for

No money. No signups. No tracking pixels. No analytics scripts. The site does not collect anything from visitors. The dossier is paid for by the citizen who built it. Every claim is checkable. The cost of sharing it is the cost of clicking "copy."
