Why it felt broken

Growth without a map

First: prove MAID on the public record (The Case). Then: investigate government more broadly (Investigations). Then: run a live desk (News). Without separate doors, every page looked the same and the investigations hub became a dump of news packages, OSINT tools, and thin stubs. That dump is closed. Thin pages are held back from the investigations shelves until they can support analysis.
