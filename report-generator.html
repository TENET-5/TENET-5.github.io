How It Works

Each MP gets a personalized link to the MP Scorecard showing their individual record. Clean MPs (score 0) get sent the full findings page — they become allies. Flagged MPs get sent the scorecard filtered to show their peers' flags — they think they have ammo but the data catches everyone.
