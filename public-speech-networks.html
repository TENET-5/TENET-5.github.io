    06 · Method

    How this board was built

    1. Primary surface: public posts and public profile text on X.
    2. Edge types: direct @-mention / reply chains; archive co-presence counts; self-bio organization links; labeled third-party public claims.
    3. Null edges: published when searches and prior banks found no bilateral public speech — a finding, not a gap to fill with rumor.
    4. Out of scope: private messages, non-public documents, doxxing, and unverified legal-name locks presented as fact.

    Related board: the institutional public-record network (lobbying, appointments, procurement). That board uses government instruments. This file uses public social speech. Do not conflate the two datasets.
