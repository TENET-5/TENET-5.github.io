    07 · Method

    How this board was built

    1. Primary surface: public posts and public profile text on X; official org web where mapped.
    2. Edge types: reply chains; archive co-presence; self-bio org links; labeled third-party claims.
    3. Null edges: published when no bilateral public speech is banked — a finding, not a rumor gap.
    4. Out of scope: private messages, non-public documents, doxxing, unverified legal-name locks as fact.

    Related board: the institutional public-record network. Do not conflate social-speech edges with government instruments.
