Promise vs Delivery

What they said. What happened. Every major program promise vs actual outcome — from official records.

MAID Safeguards

Promised (2016)

Terminal illness only. Reasonably foreseeable death. Two independent opinions. 10-day reflection. Robust safeguards.

vs

Delivered (2024)

Non-terminal eligible. Reflection period eliminated. 76,475 deaths. 5.1% of all deaths. Track 2 for disabilities. Veterans offered MAID for wheelchair ramps.

Pharmacare

Promised (2019)

Universal single-payer pharmacare. Hoskins Report: save $5B/year through bulk purchasing. Cover all essential medications.

vs

Delivered (2024)

Bill C-64: diabetes + contraception only. Full implementation: no date. 5 years from report to partial legislation. 1 in 5 still can't afford prescriptions.

Dental Care

Promised (2022)

Universal dental coverage. 9 million eligible. End dental care as a luxury for those with employer insurance.

vs

Delivered (2024-26)

Phased rollout. Provider opt-outs limit access. Processing delays. Rural gaps. Cards issued ≠ care delivered.

$10/Day Childcare

Promised (2021)

$30B over 5 years. $10/day by 2026. 250,000 new spaces. Modeled on Quebec.

vs

Delivered (2026)

Fees reduced (real achievement). But waitlists exploded. ECEs leaving — can't afford to work in childcare. Spaces didn't scale with demand.

Bill C-70: Foreign Influence Transparency Registry. Counter foreign interference. Hold agents accountable.

vs

650+ days since Royal Assent. Zero registrations. Zero enforcement actions. Zero charges. RCMP non-enforcement.

Passport Processing

Standard

20 business days. Predictable renewal cycle (10-year passports). Routine government service.

vs

Delivered (2022-23)

Months-long waits. Overnight lineups. 400K+ immigration files processed while citizens waited. AG documented: zero preparation for predictable demand.

Water Advisories

Promised (2015)

End all long-term drinking water advisories on First Nations by March 2021. $8.2B allocated.

vs

Delivered (2026)

143 lifted (real progress). 28+ remain. Target missed by 5+ years. Some communities: 20+ years without safe water.

Phoenix Pay

Promised (2009)

Modern pay system. $309M budget. Replace outdated system. Serve 300,000+ public servants.

vs

Delivered (2016-2031)

$9.3B+ total cost. 216,000 transactions backlogged. 150,000+ affected. Won't be fixed until 2031. Launched despite internal warnings.

The Pattern

Promise transformation. Set aggressive timelines. Underfund delivery. Measure by announcements, not outcomes. When the gap becomes undeniable, blame complexity. Never hold anyone accountable. Every program above follows the same pattern. This is not a series of individual failures. It is how the institution operates.
