## 0. Theory of the case (what you tell the justice in one minute)

Canada’s civilian population was subjected to a **widespread, systematic set of state acts**:

1. **Cultural and demographic demolition** — mass immigration levels on the public IRCC record, Century Initiative’s 100-million target in the open, housing and healthcare collapse on the AG/CMHC/CIHI record — while the people already here were told they were the problem. That is the cultural-genocide *frame* (TRC language + Rome 6(c) / 7(2)(b) *conditions of life*). It is not, by itself, a Form 2 count.
2. **Physical destruction of the surplus population** — Track 2 MAID (Bill C-7), Ontario coroner non-compliance, veterans offered death instead of care, jail food below the Geneva floor, COVID-era detention and mandates. These are the **killing / harm / conditions** counts.
3. **Political prosecutions of the remnant** — Emergencies Act 2022 (Federal Court: unjustified), convoy and protest process, firearms Orders in Council sold to Parliament as “not targeting law-abiding owners,” while CSIS foreign-interference warnings sat on the Public Safety minister’s desk.
4. **Foreign-state and foreign-network overlay** — NSICOP, Hogue, CSIS targeting of MPs, Mendicino’s own briefings. Deaths and destruction of life (MAID, neglected veterans, remand lockdowns, mandate-era harms) occurred **under officers who had been warned about foreign interference and who sent tax dollars out of the country** ($25.5 billion Ukraine commitment; ~$35 million/year CSC kitchen). Capacity kills “lawful excuse.”

**Protected groups for Rome 6:** national group (Canadians as such is contested); ethnical/religious subsets; **disability is not in Article 6** — TENET5’s own elements page therefore leads **Article 7** (civilian population, including disabled, poor, remand, the unvaccinated as a civilian cohort) and uses Article 6 where a national/Indigenous/religious group is the target. **Do not collapse disability-MAID into Article 6 in front of a justice.** Use Article 7 and the *Crimes Against Humanity and War Crimes Act*. Use s. 241 and s. 215 for the Form 2 that can actually issue process.
