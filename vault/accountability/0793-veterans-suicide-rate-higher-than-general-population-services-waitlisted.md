# Veterans Suicide — Rate Higher Than General Population, Services Waitlisted

**HIGHER RATE — SERVICES WAITLISTED — OFFERED MAID INSTEAD** · Ongoing

## Detail

Canadian veterans die by suicide at rates higher than the general population. Mental health services through VAC have waitlists. Some veterans who called for help were offered MAID instead of treatment (documented in Hansard). The government trains soldiers to fight, deploys them to war zones, brings them home broken, waitlists them for help, and offers them death when help takes too long. This page exists because one veteran survived the system. Many don't.

## Outcome

OUTCOME: Veteran suicide: higher than general population. Services: waitlisted. Some offered MAID instead of help. This page is the result: 791 records. Every record is evidence. The system is still broken.
