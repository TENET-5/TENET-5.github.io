# Veteran Mental Health — PTSD Untreated, VAC Wait Times, Suicide Crisis

**PTSD UNTREATED — VAC DELAYS — SUICIDE — TRANSITION GAP** · Ongoing

## Detail

Veterans with PTSD face VAC processing delays of months to years for disability claims. Operational Stress Injury clinics have waitlists. The transition gap between DND and VAC means veterans lose access to military mental health services before VAC services begin. Some veterans are offered MAID instead of treatment (Hansard, 2022). The system that breaks soldiers can't fix them. The wait for help is longer than the wait for death. And when a veteran asks for help, some VAC employees offer death instead.

## Outcome

OUTCOME: PTSD untreated. VAC delays months-years. Transition gap. MAID offered to veterans. The military breaks soldiers, releases them into a gap where no services exist, makes them wait months for help, and when they call asking for support, some employees offer death.
