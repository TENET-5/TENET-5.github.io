# Bill Blair — TPS Chief (2005–2015) → Public Safety / Emergency Preparedness / Defence Minister (2018–2025)

**CHIEF-TO-CABINET PIPELINE** · 2010–2025

## Detail

William “Bill” Blair was Chief of the Toronto Police Service from 2005 to 2015 and an MP from 2015. As TPS Chief he commanded the 2010 G20 Toronto operation — over 1,100 arrests (the largest mass detention in Canadian history), most released without charges; the Ontario Ombudsman's 2010 report Caught in the Act described the secret regulation invoked to expand police powers as tantamount to martial law. The OIPRD's 2012 systemic review of G20 policing produced no criminal sanction against the senior command. Federal cabinet portfolios followed: Border Security and Organized Crime Reduction (2018–2019), Public Safety (2019–2021 — the period that prepared the federal posture later applied to the Freedom Convoy), Emergency Preparedness (2021–2023), President of the King's Privy Council (2023–2024), and National Defence (2023–2025) under PMs Trudeau and Carney. The Mass Casualty Commission (2023) found that RCMP Commissioner Brenda Lucki “promised Public Safety Minister Bill Blair and the PMO to leverage the [Portapique mass shooting] murders to get a gun control law passed,” per Halifax Examiner reporting and four RCMP staff testifying under oath. Blair has publicly disputed the political-pressure characterization. His final cabinet posting (Defence) included CAF procurement, MAID-veteran-allegation oversight, and the 2% NATO commitment debate. Sources: Ontario Ombudsman Caught in the Act (2010); OIPRD G20 systemic review (2012); House of Commons cabinet announcements (2018, 2019, 2021, 2023); Mass Casualty Commission Final Report (2023); Halifax Examiner; [police-chiefs-canadian.html](police-chiefs-canadian.html#blair-tps-cabinet); [le-political-ties.html — Blair row](le-political-ties.html#row-blair-tps); [police-militarization.html](police-militarization.html).
