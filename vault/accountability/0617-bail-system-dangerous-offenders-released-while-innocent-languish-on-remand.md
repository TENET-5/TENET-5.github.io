# Bail System — Dangerous Offenders Released While Innocent Languish on Remand

**DANGEROUS RELEASED — INNOCENT WAREHOUSED — SYSTEM INVERTED** · Ongoing

## Detail

The bail system simultaneously releases dangerous repeat offenders — who go on to reoffend, sometimes violently — while warehousing legally innocent people on remand for months or years because they can't afford bail or don't have a surety. The system is inverted: the dangerous are released, the vulnerable are incarcerated. Judges are keeping the wrong people behind bars while releasing people who pose genuine public safety risks. The system isn't broken — it's functioning exactly as designed: to process people, not to protect them.

## Outcome

OUTCOME: Dangerous offenders released. Innocent warehoused. Can't afford bail = stay in jail. Repeat offenders released on conditions they violate. The bail system protects the rights of the dangerous and punishes the poverty of the vulnerable. It's not a justice system — it's a sorting mechanism based on wealth.
