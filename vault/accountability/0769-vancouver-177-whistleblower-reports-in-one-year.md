# Vancouver — 177 Whistleblower Reports in One Year

**177 REPORTS — 204 ALLEGATIONS — ONE CITY — ONE YEAR** · 2025

## Detail

Vancouver's Auditor General received 177 whistleblower reports in 2025 alone, containing 204 distinct allegations. 177 reports. One city. One year. If one Canadian city generates 177 whistleblower complaints in a single year, how many would the federal government generate if it had a functioning whistleblower system? The answer: it doesn't have one. Sources: Vancouver Auditor General 2025 Whistleblower Report.

OUTCOME: 177 whistleblower reports. 204 allegations. One city. One year. Vancouver has a functioning whistleblower system. The federal government does not. One system receives complaints. The other punishes the people who make them.
