# National Defence Act — 1950s Military Justice System, Unfair to Soldiers

**1950s LAW — SEPARATE JUSTICE — CHARTER GAPS — SOLDIERS UNPROTECTED** · 1950–Present

## Detail

The National Defence Act creates a separate military justice system that operates in parallel to the civilian system — with fewer Charter protections, different rules of evidence, and a chain of command that controls prosecution. Soldiers are subject to a justice system written in the 1950s. The military justice system that is supposed to protect soldiers can also be turned against them for their political opinions.
