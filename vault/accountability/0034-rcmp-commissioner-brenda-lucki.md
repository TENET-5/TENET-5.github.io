# RCMP Commissioner Brenda Lucki

**POLITICAL INTERFERENCE** · 2020

## Detail

Mass Casualty Commission found Lucki pressured Nova Scotia RCMP to release weapons details from the Portapique mass shooting (22 dead) to advance the PM's gun control legislation. Four RCMP staff testified under oath that Lucki reprimanded them for not including firearms details at a press conference. Halifax Examiner: Lucki "promised Public Safety Minister Bill Blair and the PMO to leverage the murders to get a gun control law passed." No RCMP reforms implemented after the shooting. Cross-reference: the Bill Blair record below covers the federal-cabinet end of this finding; the broader TPS-failure chain is filed at [project-south-tps.html](project-south-tps.html) (8-officer 2026 prosecution) and [missing-and-missed-review.html](missing-and-missed-review.html) (Justice Epstein's 2021 Independent Civilian Review).

## Outcome

OUTCOME: Lucki left post in 2023. No charges. No reforms. 22 Canadians dead. The RCMP's own commissioner tried to use their murders for political gain.
