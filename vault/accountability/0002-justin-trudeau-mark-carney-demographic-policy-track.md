# Justin Trudeau / Mark Carney — Demographic Policy Track

**DEMOGRAPHIC POLICY ACCOUNTABILITY** · 2015–present

## Detail

Federal Multi-Year Immigration Levels Plan signatures across the Trudeau premiership took planned annual permanent-resident admissions from approximately 285,000 (2015 inherited) to 500,000 (2025 originally planned), a ~75% increase over a single decade. The October 2024 revision back to ~395,000 for 2025 — implemented during Trudeau's final months and continued under Carney — left intake at roughly 1.5× early-Trudeau-era levels and substantially above the pre-Trudeau pre-2015 baseline. Concurrent record: CMHC's own Housing Supply Report identifies a 3.5-million-unit gap by 2030 to restore affordability to 2004 levels; Bank of Canada Monetary Policy Reports identify shelter inflation as the single largest contributor to headline CPI through 2023–2024; Statistics Canada quarterly GDP-per-capita data shows multi-quarter decline making Canada the G7 economy with the most prolonged per-capita regression in this window — Bank of Canada Senior Deputy Governor Carolyn Rogers used the term "productivity emergency." The named ministers across the trajectory: McCallum (300K plan), Hussen (300K→330K), Mendicino (first to cross 400K), Fraser (431K→465K), Miller (485K→500K and the Oct 2024 reduction to 395K). The Century Initiative target of 100M by 2100 was cited approvingly across this period without formal cabinet adoption or repudiation. Cross-reference: [canada-demographic-policy-track.html](canada-demographic-policy-track.html) for the full named-defendant track; [brookfield-maid.html](brookfield-maid.html) for the housing-collapse / MAID-Track-2 economic-floor convergence.

## Outcome

ONGOING: Trajectory continues at structurally elevated levels under Carney. CMHC supply-gap unaddressed at scale; per-capita GDP regression unrebroken at time of writing; no cabinet-level repudiation of the Century Initiative target. The dossier files this as a policy-accountability record against the named Prime Ministers and Immigration Ministers; the populations affected by the policy are not on this record.
