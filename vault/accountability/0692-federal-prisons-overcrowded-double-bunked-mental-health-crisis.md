# Federal Prisons — Overcrowded, Double-Bunked, Mental Health Crisis

**OVERCROWDED — DOUBLE-BUNKED — MENTAL HEALTH CRISIS** · Ongoing

## Detail

Federal prisons are overcrowded with widespread double-bunking. Mental health services are inadequate. Indigenous people are massively overrepresented (32% of federal inmates, 5% of population). The Correctional Investigator has documented systemic failures for decades. The same government that offers MAID to the mentally ill locks up the mentally ill in overcrowded prisons without mental health services. The correctional system — where inmates choose jail over freezing — is documented at the federal level as a systemic failure.

## Outcome

OUTCOME: Overcrowded. Double-bunked. 32% Indigenous (5% of population). Mental health: inadequate. The Correctional Investigator documents the failures. The government reads the reports. Nothing changes. The inmates choosing jail over freezing are the federal statistics in human form.
