Section 02 | Product definition

What this site is

TENET5 is operational intelligence for public accountability - a primary-source record of Canadian federal power: health policy costs in lives, procurement and defence spending, capture and foreign influence, democracy and recovery. It is not a blog, not partisan spin, and not a hardware manual. LIRIL is the AI brand that reads sources and guides the tour.

Thesis threads include mapping systemic harm to UN Genocide Convention Article II using government data, and tracking where public money and policy actually go. You do not need to accept every legal framing to use the primary documents.

Section 03 | Ground rules

Every page on this site

  • Every claim has a source - government records, court rulings, commission transcripts, statutes. No anonymous sourcing.
  • APA-style citations - bibliography surfaces on argument-sources and evidence pages.
  • Daily clarity first - the briefing answers what government is doing before deep-dive dossiers.
  • LIRIL guides, you verify - narration never replaces the primary link.
  • No owner infrastructure on the public surface - brand only; systems details stay private.
