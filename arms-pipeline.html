01 The Timeline

What the government said vs. what happened.

October 7, 2023
Hamas attack on Israel. CIJA lobbying surges 239% — Nov 2023 hits 42 communications (6x September baseline).
January 8, 2024
Government announces "pause" on new arms export permits to Israel. Presented as policy shift.
Throughout 2024
$18.9M in military goods exported to Israel using 164 pre-existing permits. Israel remains top 5 non-US destination. Categories: spacecraft ($12.5M), aircraft ($2.9M), bombs/missiles ($2.3M).
March 11, 2024
Bill C-233 (Export and Import Permits Act — arms restrictions) fails 22-295. Only NDP and Green vote yes. 9.7% Liberal dissent. CIJA lobbied against this bill.
August 2024
Minister Joly blocks sale of 50,000 mortar cartridges from General Dynamics after human rights pressure.
September 26, 2024
WEEKS LATER: Canadian Commercial Corporation signs $78.8M contract with the same company (GD-OTS-Canada) for 155mm artillery propellants routed through the US to Israel.
February 2025
$37.2M in NEW permits approved — including explosives. Contradicts the "pause."
March 20, 2025
CCC CEO writes to Minister LeBlanc flagging the GD-OTS-Canada contract. Notes GD is the "exclusive supplier" of US howitzer propellant.

02 The Contracts

Who builds the weapons, who approves them, who profits.

General Dynamics OTS-Canada

Valleyfield, Quebec | CCC-brokered | Signed Sep 26, 2024
$78.8M (US$55.1M)
155mm artillery shell propellant — the sole US supplier. The same shells were used against Palestinians in Gaza. Signed weeks after Joly blocked a smaller sale from the same company (August 2024 block → September 26, 2024 contract). Global Affairs may have attempted to scrub transfer records.

Elbit Systems (Israel)

Israeli state-connected manufacturer | Dec 2020 + Dec 2021
$44.8M combined
$36.16M for Hermes 900 StarLiner drone — a "civilian" version of the same lethal military drone used in Gaza. $8.6M for Torch X battle management system. Purchased directly from an Israeli manufacturer.

Lockheed Martin F-35

US manufacturer | Sole-source
$27.7B lifecycle
88 F-35 fighters. The Auditor General found DND understated costs by $10B+. The program was cancelled, re-competed, and then awarded to the same vendor at a higher price. The F-35 is extensively used by the Israeli Air Force in Gaza operations.

Irving Shipbuilding CSC

Irving family, Saint John NB | 2,040 lobbying comms
$84B (est. from $26.2B)
15 Type 26 frigates. PBO estimate: $84B. Design not finalized. The family donated to both the Liberal and Conservative parties. James Irving: 1,502 personal lobbying communications.

The Pattern

CIJA lobbied to maintain arms exports. The government announced a "pause" for optics. $18.9M was exported anyway via pre-existing permits. New permits were quietly approved ($37.2M). A Crown corporation signed a $78.8M contract with General Dynamics weeks after blocking a smaller sale from the same company (August 2024 block, September 26 contract). A former Defence Minister personally lobbies his old department for the Israeli weapons manufacturer. The same procurement gatekeepers are targeted by multiple competing contractors. Israel remains a top 5 Canadian military export destination despite the "pause." Total pipeline: $229M+.

The pause was a press release, not a policy.
