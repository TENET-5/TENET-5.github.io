How to read

Three questions before you open a file.

1 · What is this? Investigation = deep dossier. News = what moved today. Case = Article 6 acts.
2 · What is on the record? Prefer tables and linked primaries. Labels STATED / INFERRED stay on the page.
3 · What is not claimed? A file is not a conviction. Open the source. You verify.
